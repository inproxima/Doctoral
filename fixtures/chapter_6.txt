CHAPTER 6: CONCLUSION

6.1 Summary of Findings
This study examined teacher use of generative AI in formative assessment. It found time savings, trust concerns, and workload shifts.

6.2 Contribution to Knowledge
The study contributes the first account of this phenomenon and definitively establishes that AI improves assessment practice in secondary schools.

6.3 Recommendations
Schools should adopt AI tools. Teacher training should be provided. Governments should regulate AI in schools.

6.4 Future Research
Future research should use larger samples and quantitative methods.

6.5 Concluding Remarks
Generative AI is transforming assessment and this study has shown how.
