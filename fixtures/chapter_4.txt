CHAPTER 4: RESULTS

4.1 Overview
Three themes emerged from the analysis.

4.2 Theme 1: Time Savings
Most teachers reported saving time. One teacher said "it cuts my marking time in half". Another said it was "a huge relief".

4.3 Theme 2: Trust
Teachers were cautious about AI feedback. Many said they checked outputs. A few said they trusted it completely.

Table 1
Theme          Count
Time savings   19
Trust          16
Workload       11

4.4 Theme 3: Workload
Teachers said workload shifted rather than reduced. This suggests that AI does not truly reduce workload, which is an important finding for schools.

4.5 Summary
The results show that AI saves time but raises trust concerns.
