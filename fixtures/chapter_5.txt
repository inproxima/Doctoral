CHAPTER 5: DISCUSSION

5.1 Interpretation
The findings confirm that generative AI benefits teachers, consistent with Chen (2023) and Okafor (2023). The time savings reported here prove that AI reduces teacher workload substantially across the sector.

5.2 Trust
Teachers' caution aligns with TAM's perceived usefulness construct.

5.3 Contribution
This study makes a significant contribution to the field by being the first to examine this topic.

5.4 Implications
Schools should adopt generative AI tools for formative assessment. Policymakers should fund this.

5.5 Limitations
The sample was small. Some limitations exist.
