CHAPTER 3: METHODOLOGY

3.1 Research Design
This study used a qualitative design. Interviews were conducted with teachers.

3.2 Participants
A sample of 24 teachers was recruited. A sample of 24 was deemed sufficient. Participants were recruited from schools known to the researcher.

3.3 Instrument
A semi-structured interview protocol was developed by the researcher based on the literature. The protocol contained 12 questions.

3.4 Procedure
Interviews lasted between 20 and 60 minutes and were conducted online. They were recorded and transcribed.

3.5 Data Analysis
Thematic analysis was used. Themes were identified from the data.

3.6 Ethics
Ethics approval was obtained. Participants gave consent.
