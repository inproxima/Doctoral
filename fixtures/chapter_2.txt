CHAPTER 2: LITERATURE REVIEW

2.1 Search Strategy
I searched Google Scholar for "AI in education" and selected relevant papers. Twenty-two studies were included.

2.2 Generative AI in Education
Several studies report positive outcomes from AI use in classrooms (Chen, 2023; Okafor, 2023; Lindqvist, 2024). Chen (2023) found teachers saved time. Okafor (2023) found students engaged more. Lindqvist (2024) found improved feedback quality. All of these studies show that AI is beneficial for education.

2.3 Teacher Trust in Automated Systems
Trust has been studied in aviation and medicine. Some work suggests teachers distrust automated grading, but this is likely outdated.

2.4 Theoretical Framework
This study uses the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM). TAM says perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use predict adoption. TAM is a well-known model and is therefore appropriate here.

2.5 The Gap
As shown above, no study has examined teacher use of generative AI in formative assessment. This confirms the gap identified in Chapter 1.
